What data I collect

Depending on the form, I may collect your name, email address, message, language, source page, your newsletter/marketing preference and, when you choose it, your permission for promotional use of workshop clips or screenshots in which you may appear. I do not ask you to provide special-category or sensitive personal data through these forms.

Why I use it

  • Contact messages: to answer your enquiry and, where relevant, take steps you request before booking or purchasing a service.
  • Newsletter: to send news, workshops, mentoring updates, benefits and other information about my artistic activity when you have actively subscribed.
  • Discount or access requests: to deliver what you asked for. Promotional emails are only sent when you separately choose to receive them.
  • Workshop promotional media: if you explicitly agree, I may use short clips or screenshots from the workshop for promotional purposes on my social media. This permission is optional and can be withdrawn for future uses at any time.

Legal basis

For newsletter, promotional email and optional promotional use of workshop clips or screenshots, the legal basis is your consent. For contact enquiries, the basis is taking steps at your request before a possible contract when applicable, or my legitimate interest in answering a message that you have sent to me.

How long I keep it

Contact enquiries are normally kept for up to 12 months after the conversation is resolved, unless a customer relationship or legal obligation requires a longer period. Newsletter data is kept while your subscription remains active. Evidence of workshop media consent may be retained while it is relevant to document the permission granted and any withdrawal. If you unsubscribe or withdraw consent, I will stop the relevant future use and retain only the minimum evidence that may be legally necessary.

Service providers and international processing

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Form submissions are stored using Supabase. The primary database for these forms is hosted in Paris, France (EU). These providers may use approved subprocessors and safeguards under their data-processing terms where processing involves countries outside the European Economic Area.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing where applicable. When processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

To exercise your rights or unsubscribe, write to ramiroclemente2000@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

No automated decisions

I do not use the information submitted through these forms to make automated decisions or create profiles that produce legal or similarly significant effects.
