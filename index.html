What it is

A personal framework.

The mentorship is designed for artists with an active practice who need direction, structure and individual guidance. It does not begin with generic exercises, but with your images, interests, doubts and decisions.

It is a guided reading of your process. It is not a course or a workshop. It is a personalized guide for your creative process.

Each week, you will receive a work proposal or a line of research adapted to your needs. The goal is to help you think about your work with more precision and build a more personal visual language.

How it works

8 weeks. A clear rhythm.

The process combines initial orientation, practical work, written feedback and a final review based on your evolution.

About me

Ramiro Clemente.

Visual artist and educator based in Barcelona. I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Fine Arts and postgraduate studies in Visual Culture from the University of Barcelona.

More than 300 of my works, including drawings, collages and photography, are part of public and private collections. I have also developed illustrations for books, covers, magazines and newspapers.

I have received awards and recognition in drawing and photography competitions, and I have accompanied more than 2,000 students through online and in-person classes, mentorships and international educational projects.

My work in this mentorship is not to evaluate your work in terms of good or bad, but in terms of useful or not useful for achieving specific objectives. My role is to make a critical reading of your work, offer viable alternatives for its development based on your submissions, and propose resources, exercises and audiovisual materials that can help you make visual decisions.

Before starting

Frequently asked questions.

What is the difference between a course and a mentorship?

In a course, you follow a general structure designed for a group. In a mentorship, the work is built around your work, your needs, your questions and your evolution. The goal is not only to incorporate resources, but to apply them to your own direction.

Do I need to have my own style?

No. The mentorship is precisely a guided research and development tool for finding your own voice within the area or language you want to develop. The result will also depend on your commitment and your capacity for work.

What kind of images can I upload?

You can upload images of drawings, paintings, collages, sketchbook pages, mixed media pieces, works in progress or visual research. It is important that they are clear and have enough resolution to be evaluated properly.

How much time should I dedicate each week?

The mentorship is designed for people with an active practice. The greater your level of commitment, the more you will get from the process. My role is to offer attentive feedback on everything you submit and help you orient your work with continuity.
